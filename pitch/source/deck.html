07 · What can go wrong

What can go wrong, and how we stop it.

Wrong or foreign data

The server says no

Promises, invented amounts and another merchant’s data are refused server-side — even if the screen is bypassed.

Rubber-stamping

We watch what winning could break

Edit rate and 7-day reopens, per language. French or German reopens above 1.5 × English for 2 weeks: that language drops to suggestions.

Appendix · Experiment design

Randomise the specialist-day, size for the slowest guardrail.

StepTickets per arm
Primary · 13 % saving, SD 14.4 min~600
Reopen guardrail · 5 % → 7 %~2,210
Clustering · 12 tickets a day, ICC 0.05× 1.54 → ~3,400
Duration · 214 eligible tickets a day7 weeks
Why specialist-days

Tickets leak: drafts seen in the morning change manual answers in the afternoon. 18 specialists give only 9 per arm.

Rules set before

α 5 %, power 80 %. One read at week 7 + 7 days. Stop early only on harm. Assumptions measured in shadow mode, then frozen.
